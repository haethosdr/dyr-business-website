Why Gutter Maintenance Matters?

Gutters direct rainwater away from your home’s roof, walls, foundation, and landscape. When they’re clogged, everything from mold growth to structural damage can creep in. But with Rogers Windows and Gutters on your side, those problems never have a chance. Here’s how their expert care can save you money and headaches:

1. Preventing Foundation Damage

  • When gutters are clogged, rainwater can’t drain properly and overflows, pooling around your home’s foundation. Over time, this leads to cracks, soil erosion, and severe foundation issues.
  • With regular service from Rogers Windows and Gutters, rainwater flows freely, protecting your property’s most critical structure.

2. Stopping Roof Leaks and Rot

  • Leaves and debris left in your gutters trap moisture. That trapped water can seep under shingles, leading to rot and costly roof repairs.
  • Regular visits from Rogers Windows and Gutters keep everything clean, extending your roof’s lifespan.

3. Avoiding Mold and Pest Infestations

  • Clogged gutters create damp, stagnant environments, ideal for mold, mildew, and pest breeding. These intrusions can damage your attic, siding, and insulation.
  • With local gutter cleaning services provided by Rogers Windows and Gutters, your home stays protected from health hazards and infestations.

4. Preserving Curb Appeal and Exterior Walls

  • Overflowing water can stain walls and damage paint, leaving your home looking worn.
  • Rogers Windows and Gutters ensures your gutters stay clear, helping maintain that polished, well-kept look.

5. Preventing Ice Dams and Winter Damage

  • In colder months, trapped water can freeze, creating dangerous ice dams. These dams lift shingles and let water seep inside, leading to leaks and interior damage.
  • With the seasonal maintenance offered by Rogers Windows and Gutters, your gutters are always ready for any weather.

FAQs

1. How often should gutters be cleaned?

Gutters should be cleaned at least twice a year—once in the spring and once in the fall. Homes with nearby trees may need more frequent visits.

2. What are the signs of clogged gutters?

Watch for water spilling over the sides, sagging gutters, or plant growth inside the channels. These are all signs that you need a visit from Rogers Windows and Gutters.

3. Can clogged gutters cause interior damage?

Yes. Water that backs up into the roof or walls can lead to ceiling leaks, wall damage, and mold.

4. How long does the cleaning process take?

Most homes can be serviced in a couple of hours. Rogers Windows and Gutters works efficiently while ensuring every inch of your system is clean and fully functional.

5. Is window cleaning also available?
