Clean solar panels go a long way

Many people dont realize that clean solar panels are an essential part to making sure they properly generate power and dont slowly degrade and need replacement.
With years of experience in one of the most green tech friendly places in the country, Rogers Windows and Gutters has extensive of experience with solar panel cleaning on variety of different spaces and properties. With deionized water – which helpes to prevent stains - and our special soft-brush and carbon fiber watered fed pole, we are able to thoroughly clean solar panels in a variety of locations and configurations. When paired with a good roof cleaning, cleaning solar panels will help prevent damage occurring to them, which not be immediately evident. Solar panels power absorption is dependent on being free of debris blocking or coating the panels. They also can slowly acrue damage and wear without regular cleaning.

The Benefits of Solar Panel Cleaning

Preventing wear and degradation

Dirty solar panels can cause wear and degradation. Solar panels can develop staining or etching from long term exposure to dirt and debris. Regular cleanings will increase the longevity of your solar panels. Prevent the expensive and time consumption of replacement! 

Clean Panels generate more electricity

Clean solar panels allow more light to reach the photovoltaic cells, increasing energy production. In low light conditions, this becomes especially importaint. Dirty solar panels can loose as much as 20% of the generating capacity. 

Keep the Roof looking clean

Ensuring your solar panels are kept clean will create beauty and add aesthetic value to the roof or surface of your home. Clean panels are can be seen from a distance and well kept once, make your home look extra attractive!
