What Happens When Gutters Are Neglected?

Ignoring your gutters isn’t just risky—it can be downright costly. Clogged gutters can lead to:

  • Overflowing water that seeps into your foundation
  • Sagging or damaged gutters from excess weight
  • Unsightly streaks or stains on your home’s siding
  • A cozy home for pests like mosquitoes or rodents

Atherton’s mix of seasonal winds and falling leaves means your gutters can fill up fast. That’s where we step in to help.

When’s the Right Time to Clean Your Gutters?

Most homes in Atherton benefit from at least two cleanings a year—once in the spring and again in the fall. However, if your property has large trees nearby or experiences heavy storms, you might need extra attention.

Waiting too long can lead to clogged gutters overflowing during the next rainstorm, which could damage your foundation or landscaping. So, it’s always better to get ahead of the problem.
